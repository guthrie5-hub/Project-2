# Archived committee memo

Built with AI help; education, not investment advice.

## Recommendation

**Pilot, not adopt** the all-month historical-estimate mix: 30% VTI, 5% VXUS, 0% BND, and 65% SGOV. It is the lowest-volatility five-point long-only mix found by the model that meets the 6.5% expected-return objective and 3% reserve minimum. Its estimated return is 6.54%, estimated volatility is 5.47%, and reserve-eligible weight is 65%.

## Why this is conditional

The recommendation depends on historical mean returns from October 2021 through September 2026. Changing only VTI's expected return from 12.00% to 8.00% changes the selected mix to 10% VTI, 40% VXUS, 0% BND, and 50% SGOV; estimated volatility rises to 7.50%. The model therefore identifies a feasible historical-estimate allocation, not a reliable forecast or a proof of superior after-cost performance.

## Baseline and mandate context

The Meridian training benchmark reaches its return objective but has no reserve-eligible assets, so it fails the reserve rule. Equal weight and inverse volatility satisfy stronger reserve exposure but miss the 6.5% return objective. The Project 2 mix meets its own stated 3% SGOV reserve constraint; the full checklist is visible in the app.

## Monitoring and reversal trigger

**Monitor:** the historical-return assumptions, especially VTI, and the relevance of the all-month versus trailing-36-month window. **Owner:** portfolio owner. **Reversal trigger:** defer or reoptimize if the lower VTI assumption is better supported, if the trailing-36-month result remains more decision-relevant, or if the objective/reserve constraint changes. The model cannot establish that the selected mix will beat a baseline after costs.
