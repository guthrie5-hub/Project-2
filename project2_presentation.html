Mandate and decision boundary

Committee question

Can the committee adopt a lowest-volatility portfolio process that meets the stated return objective and reserve rule?

Mandate

Seven-year horizon; 6.5% annual gross expected-return objective; long-only weights; at least 3% in cash-like SGOV.

Universe and benchmark

VTI, VXUS, BND, and SGOV. The transparent benchmark is 60% VTI and 40% BND.

Important boundary

The objective is gross of costs. The app cannot establish future net-of-cost performance from historical returns.

From price data to a constrained answer

Monthly adjusted closes → monthly returns → annualized arithmetic mean return, volatility, and correlations → five-point long-only mixes → objective and reserve checks → lowest-volatility valid mix

What changed

The early work demonstrated the search on training inputs. The final app adds the user’s ETF price file, baselines, constraint verdicts, point-in-time evidence, and stress cases.

Return definition

Return estimates equal the arithmetic mean monthly return multiplied by 12. They are historical estimates, not CAGRs or forecasts.

Price inputs: VTI, VXUS, BND, and SGOV monthly adjusted closes, October 2021 to September 2026. Source and data card: my_portfolio_mandate.md.

Video 2 · Technical walkthrough

Portfolio engine

Search logic

`search_lowest_risk_mix` enumerates long-only allocations in five-percentage-point steps. It rejects a mix below the return objective or reserve floor.

For every remaining mix, the engine calculates covariance from volatility and correlation, then keeps the lowest-volatility answer.

Video 3 · Conditions and monitoring

Paper pilot with explicit stop conditions

Committee action

Paper pilot with 0% funded capital. Do not adopt a live allocation until net-of-cost return assumptions support the 6.5% objective.

Reference scenario span

VTI 5–40%; VXUS 0–40%; BND 0%; SGOV 50–85%. These are sensitivity results, not simultaneous recommended weights.

Monitor

  • Return estimates and the sample window
  • Implementation costs
  • BAA vintage compliance
  • Changes to objective or reserve rule

Stop or reoptimize if

Costs prevent the objective, the 70% reserve floor becomes relevant, or the point-in-time rule cannot be followed.
